	Pros: 
		1. Salaries on time. Lack of funds is never a problem here.
		2. Good for those who live in Ambala and nearby towns, who don't want to leave their hometown.
		3. Low to Medium work pressure. Quite laid back environment.
		4. Good for freshers. They prefer freshers more than the experienced ones. Especially for engineers.
		5. Timings are not very strict.
		6. Fixed yearly increments.


	Cons: 
		Too many of them
		1. No growth in position. You start from one position in the company and most probably work at same position till retirement, only your salary will increase every year. Because they don't have any hierarchy. The MD himself is controlling everything and taking every single decision.
		2. It's a family business, not a company. Be it sales heads, plant heads, or head of any department, it will be some cousin or some distant relative of the MD. And most of them are arrogant and stupid and don't know how to behave, except a few who are down to earth and sensible.
		3. No R&amp;D. Only reverse engineering and copy pasting from rival companies.
		4. Laughably stupid rules (although hardly anyone follows them).
		5. No facilities for employees. Employee welfare is a joke in this company. No PF, no medical insurance, no canteen, no heaters in winter, no tea/coffee for office employees, you can't even bring your own food/snacks inside the office.
		6. Especially for mechanical engineers, you may be working in production, R&amp;D and design or you may be working in biomedical labs that you don't have any idea about, or you may be working here as a clerk, or working under a sales head to write mails on their behalf to the customers. They can fit you in any role. So before joining get your job responsibilities clear from in the interview itself.
		7. Very Orthodox thinking of the management. Even if you have worked for years here and when you leave, they'll treat you like you have done some fraud, they won't give you your experience letter easily.
		7. No Cash Bonuses on festivals like diwali. They'll give you some stupid crockery items worth Rs 700-800 every year.
		8. No individual performance tracking. You may be working with twice of sincerity compared to your fellow colleagues who are on the same level as you, but the MD will never get to know that and finally increments of both of you would be same. There seems no point to work hard or do something better at work. It's not going to bear any fruit


	Summary: 
		Read the Pros and Cons and decide yourself
