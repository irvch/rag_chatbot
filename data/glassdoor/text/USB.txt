		Callers can very rude - but unless they get particularly nasty, you just kind of have to eat it.
		
		The programs/systems we use for the job are incredibly unreliable. One vital system is literally from decades ago and generally cannot be trusted to work two hours in a row, let alone two days.
		
		While you can move within the company, you do have to have been in your current position for about a year or so. So, again, if you don't like where you are, you just have to bear it for a while.
		
		This isn't so much towards the position itself as it is for U.S. Bank as a whole. I've had numerous callers come in and ask for something I/the bank cannot give them - and when I ask who told them that, they will almost always say "the branch". I don't know how branch bankers are trained, but there is a big disconnect. It causes confusion for the customer and headaches for phone reps.
