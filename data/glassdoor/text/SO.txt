	Pros: 
		Over my time working at Southern Telecom, I have found it to be an overwhelmingly positive work environment with enjoyable coworkers, stimulating tasks/projects, and generally strong rapport. People invest themselves in what they do, and most interactions that I observe between team members of various departments are productive and pleasant. I can honestly say that I generally enjoy being at work, which is largely due to the good relationship that I have with my direct supervisor, but is also due to the good relationships I have with my colleagues and to the stimulating work environment. The office is also bright and fairly open, which helps to prevent feelings of dreariness/grogginess, even on darker or rainy days.


	Cons: 
		A reality of working at this company, for better or worse, is that it's very old-school in terms of work culture. With few exceptions, remote work (either fully or in a hybrid capacity) has not been embraced following the COVID shutdown, and most (not all) upper management is wary of providing employees with too much flexibility out of a concern that work will not be completed. Additionally, leadership is (as is very common among old-school leaders) often highly resistant to change that, while painful, might be necessary to improve processes or the employee experience. I hesitate to describe this culture as a pure con, since I experience it as more of a workstyle preference which either will or will not fit well for each individual. My personal preferred work culture would be one that is more in-line with contemporary research and understanding of what drives and rewards employee motivation, and that wholeheartedly adopts new workplace practices that are becoming selling points at a growing number of other organizations. With that being said, there's a lot of heart here, and I have truly learned and grown a lot as a result of performing my role as a member of this company. In spite of the things I would change about the culture, there are many practices and outlooks that I will carry from here with me whenever I do choose to move on to another organization in the future.


	Summary: 
		Updated Review After Almost 2 Years With the Company (Original Review Written About 6 Months In)
