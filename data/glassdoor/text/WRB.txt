	Cons: 
		I have worked at this company for several years and this is not easy for me. I have always tried to find the positive in everything and have always been a team player, but there comes a time when you have to be honest. This company has transitioned to a new leader as of April 2023 and it doesn't appear that he cares about the employees and the sole focus is on business. While we all certainly understand we are in the business of making money, that doesn't or shouldn't come at the cost of the mental health of your employees. We went from having a much more flexible WFH policy to one that was less flexible after COVID and now, as of a recent announcement a couple of weeks ago, we are going to a much more rigid policy, which essentially offers only one day. To add insult to injury, as we voiced our concerns about the policy what we got back is "We want people that want to be here." What kind of leader who understand the value and importance of people says that? Another ill conceived remark was , and I am paraphrasing but this is pretty accurate, "I am not giving people a Mon or Fri WFH day, because I don't believe employees should have a long weekend." Who says that? Someone who is disconnected from their employees. Comments like these, even if you think it, have no place being said and certainly should not be in the workplace. We just lived through a time where people lost their lives to a pandemic and there is a premium placed on quality of life, yet some employers don't get it. Sure, they will walk around and smile at you and authorize a FUN event every now and again, but that is it. When it comes to understanding the needs of the staff, there is no understanding. WFH helps parents, caregivers, students and even employees who may be battling a health issue and while we understand we have jobs to do, that should not mean that our lives don't have value. It should not mean that we are only numbers. Most of these decisions are made by older males, of a certain group who can't fathom that life doesn't revolve around a job. We are supposed to work to live, not live to work. What's worse, is that somehow when we ask to be treated with some basic understanding of the quality of our lives and how balance is important and can help, we are made to feel like we are ungrateful, unprofessional and that we are complainers. There is no value for the lives of the employees anymore here. When you bring up WFH, you are labeled as not being a team player, or told "We want people that want to be here." As if just asking for some balance means that we don't want to be here. This is the difference between new school leadership and old school thinking and many of those leaders that think like this, are so disconnected from employees many level under them nor do they understand the importance of those employees. Many of those leaders have no understanding of what true leadership is. This company has gone downhill since a very kind man who was the often called the "conscious" of our previous leader, left for another opportunity. He was a good man who left about two years ago.  We have a new company leader of this office and while you always want to give people a chance, you can already see three months in, that how employees feel is at the bottom of the list. People are literally coming in now and smiling and acting like things are fine and quietly looking for jobs. We have already lost some employees and I am certain we will lose many others.
