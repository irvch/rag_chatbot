	Cons: 
		With all due respect...
		
		COVID took a toll on AvisBudget Group. Many long-serving employees I encountered, with 15 years or more of tenure, seemed burned out, reflecting in their attitudes. Those who didn't display this burnout often seemed insincere, in my view.
		
		Morale is at an all-time low, with seasoned employees feeling burned out, leading to toxic and negative behavior. Positive conversations about the company are rare during casual discussions.
		
		To their credit, AvisBudget tries to boost morale with occasional "appreciation" days. However, my group perceived these efforts as artificial and insincere.
		
		Certain employees who should retire are still present. They've mastered using Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs) to dismiss new employees who challenge their tenure or don't align with their management style.
		
		HR's interventions are minimal, and they have procedures that allow managers to target and justify terminations easily.
		
		Again, I express this with all due respect...
		
		If you anticipate a fair and constructive Mediation session or PIP, you'll likely be disappointed. If they decide they want you out, you'll be let go regardless of your dedication. The company prioritizes metrics over its employees.
		
		I felt undervalued and unappreciated during my time there.
		
		Challenges and setbacks are part of life. It's a continuous learning process; either you grasp the lesson or step away.
		
		AvisBudget must learn from my departure, or they risk repeating similar issues.
		
		Once more, I share these thoughts with all due respect.
