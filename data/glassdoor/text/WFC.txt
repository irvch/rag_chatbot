		The only other major issue is that the teller position is being phased out fairly quickly. The model in the banking industry is shifting to universal banker, where branches only have bankers there to do both banking and teller transactions. So, if you start as a teller, plan on switching to banker in short order. (I'd make the same call if it were my business to run), but I feel it's only fair to mention that because of the transition I have been moved between three different branches over this past year as branches have been switched to universal banker branches.
