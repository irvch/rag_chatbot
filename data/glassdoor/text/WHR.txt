This is because talent is vetted and put through a ringer when it comes to promotions. Directors are only appointed with C-suite buy-in so that tells you a lot about how seriously they take career development. 		2) One of the few companies where you can join as an intern and become a COO or EVP. They prefer in-bred executives for key positions. This allows meritocracy that needs to be earned. 
		3) You can expect to learn a lot about best-in-class American manufacturing.

		6) Yes there are lay-offs every 5-6 years but it's targeted towards bottom performers and people who are not willing to grow in their roles. So do not be alarmed by the lay-off news, if you do good work management will work in your favor. 
		7) Culture is of essence, therefore, interview vetting is more on fit than technical aspects given they want people to join and have a long career. Avg. tenure is at least 5 years. 
		8) A lot of people live in St. Joseph a beach town which is lively during summer. Summer Fridays and even option to work M-Th and take Fri off if you're on top of your work. 
		9) Lastly, this can be a company where you can rise to the top but if you want to just chill, there are roles that allow you to do so as well and there are people who are happy doing so. The affordability of the town makes it possible.


	Cons: 
		This isn't a con necessarily but if you're young location can be a hard sell. Therefore, really think what matters to you when it comes to job, environment and location.
