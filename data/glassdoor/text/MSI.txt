		This is my first Glassdoor review ever and I am VERY honored to illustrate it with all viewers. Everyone at Motorola Solutions is genuinely passionate about what we do here. We each play an integral part in the organization and move to the same beat. I've worked with upper and middle management including peers on the ground floor and everyone here at MSI has been very helpful and welcoming. No one has shown an ounce of rank/title/superiority over another and have treated everyone with the utmost respect and inclusivity. Work is not trivial and is taxing to an extent but in a feasible manner. Work/Life balance is on point. Management sends weekly surveys to check the pulse of its employee's satisfaction (this is genius as it prevents cancer from growing). Company promotes 10% days for you to continuously learn and evolve your craft in your work life. This is a simple notion but very effective. Management continuously encourages troops to take as much time off as they can as long as it's appropriate to do so. They have inhouse mentorship programs which I've been fortunate to take part in. Good to have comradity with other individuals that have veteran experience and have been through the ups/downs of an organization. They don't guide your path but show you the forks in the road so you can avoid them. All of these company attributes just portray to employees that management truly cares about your evolution in career, knowledge and physical well being.
		
		The CEO, Greg Brown is one of the most compassionate, empathetic and "good hearted" leaders that I've ever had the privilege to listen to. His All Hands and Town Hall meetings are very informative, unscripted, raw/unfiltered which is truly a breath of fresh air. The Senior Management Team's transparency is very open which is unheard of in the tech corporate world. There is a reason why the Senior Leadership Team is not constantly churning like other organizations that I've lived through. They are all cut from the same cloth which is powerful to witness.
		
		Finally the products/solutions... This ALONE gets me excited to wake up in the morning. We are using technology to save lives and "solve for safer." This is a powerful mission and brings meaning to an employee. The engineering, support, marketing, ops and sales teams are all a bonus on top. I haven't seen any rivalries or animosity towards any departments. This is again, unheard of. We champion one another and advocate for greatness. There is no competition that I've witnessed among peers. It's clear from our CSAT and NPS scores that we provide white glove treatment to every customer that comes under our umbrella. Our brand name is evident and I see this company sticking around forever. Again, I don't see this as a "job" and on the flipside nor do I see this as a "family" (hate when organizations say that) but I will say it comes as close as ever to one... GO MSI!!!


	Cons: 
		No company is perfect but if I were to criticize anything it would be base salary and total compensation. We are paid significantly less than major tech companies (this is all public information). Granted we don't bring in the revenue like the METAs, Amazons and Googles of the world bring which is most likely the reason for the below average compensation but the company culture, management, work/life balance and most importantly the mission make up for the salary.
