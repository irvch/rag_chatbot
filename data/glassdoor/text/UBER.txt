	Pros: 
		Uber Carshare is a scaling business. You have the excitement of working in a start-up environment that is growing globally. There is a lot of "build it as you fly it" attitude, like a true start-up, but with the backing of the Uber name. The team are open and driven. Performance is genuine and rewarded. You are given a lot of space to do your work but live your life around it. Flexibility of time, location and thought is encouraged and celebrated. Everyone across the team is very available for one another. Ideas are welcome and humour is always appreciated.


	Cons: 
		Being a start-up environment, going through huge changes is not for everyone. I am on a 2-year contract, which is common in the current environment. Once again, not for everyone. The Carshare team and the Uber team run as very separate teams, cultures and business units. Not necessarily a negative but also not necessarily a positive. None of what is above is negative, just walk in with your eyes wide open and know what is right for you. You have the opportunity to create your own story and leave your own legacy.


	Summary: 
		Exciting, challenging and fun
