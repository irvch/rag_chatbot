	Pros: 
		- Whether you're working at the Gate or by the Main Stage, you're being paid to be at Live Entertainment Shows!
		- Crew Appreciation Nights: Not every show, but sometimes you get free food after or before some shows
		- After Season Appreciation Day: At the venue I work at (Darien Lake Amphitheater), it's connected to a Theme Park and at the Season end, we all get to bring a friend for free to go to the Theme Park after a little Company Picnic thing
		- Pay is decent, if you live near the Venue.. I do not though so it's really just about seeing my favourite bands for free honestly. Concert tickets are more expensive than the gas I spend to get there lol (it's only maybe a half hour or so drive, anyway)
		- If you work Security, there's more travel opportunities to other Venues. Usher/Ticket Takers also have travel opportunities but not as often. This is a pro because you get paid for the travel time too, and it's usually a bus so you don't have to even drive. Paid to sleep on the bus on the way there and back to the home venue! Lol
		- Some opportunity for growth.. if you seek it out. You won't get promoted unless you're applying for positions specifically through their website.


	Cons: 
		- Pay is barely above minimum wage, and I've been there 5 years and have only gotten a raise when minimum wage went up.
		
		- No benefits, besides getting paid to "see" concerts. If you're not working "in house" though, you don't get to see anything and it's not like you can just stand and watch like a paying customer either. I'd still count this as a pro, but I also am 95% of the Season "in house". If I was at a Gate the whole time, every show, I may not feel this way. Although I do still enjoy being at a Gate too. I don't enjoy every type of music lol perhaps it'd be nice if people could at the beginning of the year star their favs so management could keep in mind where to place people, if staffing and staff experience levels allow that possible.
		
		- Bi-Weekly pay!!!! I absolutely HATE bi-weekly pay. This shouldn't be a thing.
		
		- Sometimes your hours can be messed up so check your time cards each week! A lot of the time it's because you forgot to clock in or out but sometimes it's the system. I've only had this issue once personally, but I know some others who have too. Management is (usually) quick to correct though so it's not a huge deal.
		
		- Training is subpar.. It's not really management's fault though..? A lot is on the job, in the moment, learning so if you're not a fast learner, I don't recommend working this type of job.


	Summary: 
		Get Paid To Listen To Your Favs
