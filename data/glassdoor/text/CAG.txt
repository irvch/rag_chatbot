	Pros: 
		You will likely work on large brands that future employers will recognize.


	Cons: 
		The brand organization is very hierarchical. Brand Managers are taught to never challenge positions above them (Directors, VPs, Division President) leading to all business strategy dictated top down. Successful brand professionals succeed by predicting what their leader wants to see vs forming a POV based on their own detailed analysis. This is good for some people but if you’re desiring to have hint of a creative/entrepreneurial spirit in or day to day role this is not the place to be. Decision making is slow because executive’s management style force VPs to micro manage every detail of their teams routine tasks down to the analyst level tasks. The c-suite must approve every decision made no matter how small and immaterial. The organization spends very little time understanding the consumer and more time on identifying voids in large demand spaces. This strategy seems to work for Conagra but leaves the employee with an under developed skillset and disadvantaged when interviewing for positions outside the company.

		Extremely hierarchical organization
