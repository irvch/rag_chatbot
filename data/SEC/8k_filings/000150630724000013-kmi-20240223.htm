Item 7.01.  Regulation FD Disclosure.

In accordance with General Instruction B.2. of Form 8-K, the following information shall not be deemed “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended, nor shall it be deemed incorporated by reference in any filing under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended.

On February 23, 2024, Kinder Morgan, Inc. (“KMI”) announced that representatives of KMI intend to participate in the following investor meetings to discuss the business and affairs of KMI:
•Scotiabank Energy & Power Conference on February 28, 2024
•Raymond James Institutional Investor Conference on March 5, 2024
•Barclays Investment Grade Energy & Pipeline Corporate Access Day on March 6, 2024
•Barclays Midstream Corporate Access Day on March 7, 2024

Materials to be used during the events will be available before 8:00 a.m. Central Time on February 26, 2024, on KMI’s website at: https://ir.kindermorgan.com/events-and-presentations/default.aspx. The live presentation for the Raymond James Institutional Investor Conference is scheduled to begin at 10:25 a.m. Eastern Time on March 5, 2024, and will also be accessible by audio webcast (both live and on-demand) on KMI’s website at the same web address. An archived webcast will remain available for 90 days on KMI’s website at the above address.
