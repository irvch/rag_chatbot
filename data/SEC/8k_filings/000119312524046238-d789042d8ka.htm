Explanatory Note

This Amendment No. 1 to Current Report on Form 8-K/A is being filed by ON Semiconductor Corporation (the “Corporation”) solely for the purpose of supplementing Item 5.02 of that certain Current Report on Form 8-K filed on August 23, 2023 (the “Original Form 8-K”) to include additional information about board committee assignments.

 
Item 5.02.

Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangement of Certain Officers

As previously reported in the Original Form 8-K, Christina Lampe-Önnerud was appointed by the board of directors of the Corporation (the “Board”) as a new non-employee director effective September 1, 2023. On February 21, 2024, the Board appointed Ms. Lampe-Önnerud to serve as a member of its Audit Committee.
