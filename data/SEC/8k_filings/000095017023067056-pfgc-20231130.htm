Item 5.07. Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

On November 30, 2023, Performance Food Group Company (the “Company”) held its 2023 Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the “Annual Meeting”). At the Annual Meeting, stockholders voted on the matters disclosed in the Company’s Definitive Proxy Statement on Schedule 14A filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on October 10, 2023 (the “Proxy Statement”). The final voting results for the matters submitted to a vote of stockholders were as follows:
