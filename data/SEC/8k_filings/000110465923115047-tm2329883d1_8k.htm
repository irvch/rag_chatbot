Item 5.03 Amendments to Articles of Incorporation or Bylaws; Change in Fiscal Year.

 

On November 6, 2023, the Board of Directors of Waste Management, Inc. (the “Company”) approved and adopted amended and restated by-laws of the Company (the “Amended and Restated By-laws”). The Amended and Restated By-laws update the procedural mechanics and disclosure requirements in connection with stockholder proposals under Section 2.13 “Stockholder Proposals” and stockholder nominations under Section 2.14 “Nomination of Directors” including, without limitation, to require a stockholder’s notice to include certain information with respect to the affiliates and associates of the stockholder giving the notice and the beneficial owner on whose behalf the nomination or proposal is made and to require certain additional disclosures from such parties. The Amended and Restated By-laws also amend Section 2.14 to require that any stockholder soliciting proxies from other stockholders must use a proxy card color other than white.

 

Additionally, the Amended and Restated By-laws include revisions to align with recent amendments to the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware, to clarify the applicable voting standards when a separate class vote of stockholders is required and a different or minimum vote is provided by applicable statute, the certificate of incorporation, by-laws or regulation; to make the Amended and Restated By-laws gender neutral; and to make other ministerial or conforming changes.

 

The above description is qualified in its entirety by reference to the Amended and Restated By-Laws, a copy of which is filed as Exhibit 3.2 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.
