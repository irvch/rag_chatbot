Item 5.07Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

 

On November 10, 2022, Summit Healthcare REIT, Inc. (the “Company”) held its annual meeting of stockholders. The matters voted upon were (1) the re-election of Messrs. J. Steven Roush and Kent Eikanas and Ms. Suzanne Koenig as directors, and (2) the approval, on a non-binding advisory basis, of compensation paid to the named executive officers.

 

(1)Each of our directors was re-elected to serve for a one-year term expiring in 2023. The final results of the voting were as follows:
