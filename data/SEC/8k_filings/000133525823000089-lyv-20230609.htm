EXPLANATORY NOTE

This current report on Form 8-K/A (this “Amendment”) amends the current report on Form 8-K dated June 9, 2023 filed by Live Nation Entertainment, Inc. (the “Company”) with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the “Original Form 8-K”). The Original Form 8-K reported the final voting results of the Company's 2023 Annual Meeting of Stockholders held on June 9, 2023 (the “2023 Annual Meeting”). The sole purpose of this Amendment is to disclose the Company's decision regarding how frequently it will conduct future stockholder advisory votes to approve the compensation of the Company's named executive officers (“say on pay”). No other changes have been made to the Original Form 8-K.

Item 5.07    Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

As previously reported in the Original Form 8-K, in a non-binding advisory vote on the frequency of future say on pay votes held at the 2023 Annual Meeting, 111,184,561 shares voted for one year, 30,586 shares voted for two years, 87,856,474 shares voted for three years, 72,491 shares abstained and there were 6,362,544 broker non-votes. After considering the voting results and other factors, the Company’s Board of Directors determined at a meeting held on September 28, 2023 that the Company will hold future say on pay votes on an annual basis, in accordance with the preference of the Company’s stockholders, at least through the occurrence of the next advisory vote on the frequency of say on pay votes. The next advisory vote regarding the frequency of say on pay votes is required to occur no later than the Company's 2029 Annual Meeting of Stockholders.
