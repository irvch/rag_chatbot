Item 8.01 Other Events.

First Quarter Dividend

In a press release dated February 20, 2024, the Company announced that the Board declared a quarterly dividend of $0.68 cents per share for the first quarter of 2024. The dividend will be payable on Wednesday, March 27, 2024 to the holders of Company common stock and on Thursday, March 28, 2024 to the holders of the Company's Swedish Depository Receipts. The dividend payments will be made to holders of record on the close of business on Tuesday, March 12, 2024.

A copy of the press release dated February 20, 2024 regarding the first quarter dividend is filed as Exhibit 99.1 to this report and is incorporated herein by reference.
