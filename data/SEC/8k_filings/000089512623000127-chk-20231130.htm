Item 8.01 Other Events.

On November 30, 2023, Chesapeake Energy Corporation (“Chesapeake”) completed the sale of its remaining Eagle Ford assets to SilverBow Resources Operating LLC, a subsidiary of SilverBow Resources, Inc. (the “Eagle Ford Sale Transaction”). As previously announced in a Form 8-K filed on August 14, 2023, the Eagle Ford Sale Transaction has an economic effective date of February 1, 2023 and represents approximately 42,000 net acres and approximately 540 wells, along with related property, plant and equipment.
