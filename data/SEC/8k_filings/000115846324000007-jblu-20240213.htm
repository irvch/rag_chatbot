Item 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure.
On February 13, 2024, JetBlue Airways Corporation (“JetBlue”) issued a press release regarding the matters described in Item 8.01 of this Current Report on Form 8-K. A copy of the press release is furnished herewith as Exhibit 99.1.
The information included under this Item 7.01 (including Exhibit 99.1) shall not be deemed “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”), or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that section, nor shall it be deemed incorporated by reference in any filing under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “Securities Act”), or the Exchange Act, except as may be expressly set forth by specific reference in such filing.
Item 8.01 Other Events.
On February 13, 2024, JetBlue announced, in connection with the Agreement and Plan of Merger, dated as of July 28, 2022, by and among JetBlue, Sundown Acquisition Corp., and Spirit Airlines, Inc. (“Spirit”), the record and payment dates for the February 2024 additional prepayment to stockholders of Spirit of $0.10 per share of Spirit common stock (the “February 2024 Additional Prepayment”). JetBlue has set February 23, 2024, as the record date for the February 2024 Additional Prepayment, with the payment of the February 2024 Additional Prepayment to occur on February 29, 2024.
