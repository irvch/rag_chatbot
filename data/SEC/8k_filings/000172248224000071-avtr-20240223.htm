Item 5.03 Amendments to Articles of Incorporation or Bylaws; Change in Fiscal Year.
The Board of Directors (the “Board”) of Avantor, Inc. (the “Company”) adopted the fourth amended and restated bylaws of the Company (the “Bylaws”) on February 23, 2024. The amendments (a) revise Section 2.03 with respect to nominations of directors and solicitations of proxies, including compliance with Rule 14a-19 under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended, (b) remove the requirement for the Company to produce and keep for examination by any stockholder during a meeting of stockholders a complete list of the stockholders entitled to vote at such meeting, which conforms to amendments to the Delaware General Corporation Law, (c) revise Section 7.01 to clarify the circumstances in which the Company is required to indemnify or reimburse expenses for an “indemnitee” (as defined in the Bylaws) and (d) incorporate ministerial, clarifying and conforming changes.
The foregoing summary does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by the Bylaws filed as Exhibit 3.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated herein by reference.
