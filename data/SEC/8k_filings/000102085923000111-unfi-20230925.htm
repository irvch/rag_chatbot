Item 5.02    Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers;         Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.

(d) Effective September 28, 2023, Lynn S. Blake, James M. Loree and James C. Pappas (the “New Directors”) were appointed to the Board of Directors of United Natural Foods, Inc. (the “Company”). The election of the New Directors was reported under Item 5.02 of the Company’s Current Report on Form 8-K filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on September 26, 2023.

The Company is filing this Form 8-K/A to disclose that on November 28, 2023, the Board appointed Ms. Blake to serve as a member of the Audit Committee and the Nominating and Governance Committee; Mr. Loree to serve as a member of the Audit Committee; and Mr. Pappas to serve as a member of the Compensation Committee and the Nominating and Governance Committee, with each appointment effective immediately. The Board has determined that each of the New Directors is independent pursuant to the New York Stock Exchange listing standards and Rule 10A-3 under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended.
