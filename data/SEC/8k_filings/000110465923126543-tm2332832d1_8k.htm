ITEM 5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.

 

(d)       On December 15, 2023, the Board of Directors (the "Board") of Ameren Corporation ("Ameren") elected Ms. Kimberly J. Harris to the Board, with a term beginning on January 1, 2024, and expiring at Ameren's Annual Meeting of Shareholders in 2024. Also effective January 1, 2024, the Board appointed Ms. Harris to the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee and the Nuclear, Operations and Environmental Sustainability Committee of the Board. No arrangement or understanding exists between Ms. Harris and Ameren or, to Ameren's knowledge, any other person or persons pursuant to which Ms. Harris was selected as a director. Ms. Harris will receive compensation for Board and committee service as described on Exhibit 10.6 to Ameren's Annual Report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2021.

 

Ms. Harris is the retired President and Chief Executive Officer of Puget Energy, Inc., an energy services holding company, and its subsidiary Puget Sound Energy, Inc., a utility company providing electric and natural gas service in the northwest United States. She served in these positions from March 2011 until her retirement in January 2020. Ms. Harris served as President of Puget Energy and Puget Sound Energy from July 2010 through February 2011 and as Executive Vice President and Chief Resource Officer from May 2007 until July 2010. Prior to joining Puget Energy, Ms. Harris practiced law at Perkins Coie LLP.

 

Ms. Harris had no business relationships with Ameren or its subsidiaries in 2022 or to the date hereof in 2023 which are required to be reported under Item 404(a) of the Securities and Exchange Commission Regulation S-K.

 

ITEM 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure.

 

On December 18, 2023, Ameren issued a press release announcing Ms. Harris's election to the Board. A copy of that press release is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to this Report.
