Item 8.01

Other Events

On August 24, 2023, The Charles Schwab Corporation (“CSC”) issued $1,350,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 6.136% Fixed-to-Floating Rate Senior Notes due 2034 and $1,000,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 5.875% Senior Notes due 2026 (collectively, the “Notes”). The net proceeds of the offering of the Notes were approximately $2,330,800,000, after deducting underwriting discounts and commissions and estimated offering expenses.

The Notes were issued under a Senior Indenture, dated as of June 5, 2009, between CSC and The Bank of New York Mellon Trust Company, N.A., as trustee, as supplemented by the Twenty-First Supplemental Indenture, dated as of May 19, 2023 and the Twenty-Third Supplemental Indenture, dated as of August 24, 2023 (the “Twenty-Third Supplemental Indenture”). The offering was made pursuant to the prospectus supplement dated August 22, 2023 and the accompanying prospectus dated December 4, 2020, filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission (“SEC”) pursuant to CSC’s effective registration statement on Form S-3 (File No. 333-251156) (the “Registration Statement”).

On August 22, 2023, CSC entered into an Underwriting Agreement (the “Underwriting Agreement”) with BofA Securities, Inc., Citigroup Global Markets Inc., Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC, J.P. Morgan Securities LLC, Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC and Wells Fargo Securities, LLC, as representatives of the several underwriters named therein (collectively, the “Underwriters”), pursuant to which CSC agreed to issue and sell the Notes to the Underwriters.

Copies of (a) the Underwriting Agreement, (b) the Twenty-Third Supplemental Indenture, (c) the form of 6.136% Fixed-to-Floating Rate Senior Notes due 2034, (d) the form of 5.875% Senior Notes due 2026, and (e) a validity opinion with respect to the Notes are attached as Exhibits 1.1, 4.82, 4.83, 4.84 and 5.1, respectively, to this Current Report on Form 8-K and are incorporated by reference into the Registration Statement.
